# Third-Party Services and Dependencies

AETHRION may rely on hosting, backend/database services, AI providers, local-model software, libraries and browser APIs. Known project context includes Vercel, Back4App/Parse Cloud Code, external AI APIs (including NVIDIA-hosted models where enabled), Ollama where configured, and browser speech recognition/synthesis where supported.

This is not a complete live software bill of materials and does not prove every service is active. Verify integrations, SDK versions, provider terms and privacy settings before release. Keep provider secrets server-side, explain relevant data transfers and do not claim prompts never leave the device unless the architecture proves it.
